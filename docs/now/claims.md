# Claims and sessions

- Claim with `muster next` or `muster claim <id>` from inside the session, so it carries the stamp. (PROTOCOL.md "Sessions")
- One claim per holder: a second unblocked task in Building is refused. (mu-2)
- The owner may hold several claims. (mu-2)
- A task sent back from Testing goes to its builder and comes first, even beside another claim. (mu-66)
- Claims are checked under the queue's lock: of two at once, one wins. (mu-2)
- muster stamps the holder (label, checkout, session, account); don't type it. (mu-30)
- A holder is one label in one session and one agent; a teammate passes `--agent <id>`. (mu-90)
- `--role coder` on claim, next or start hands out the role's lowest free slot (`Kaveh-2`); act as it with `--as Kaveh-2` from then on. (mu-112)
- One session is one slot: beside a slot's claim it holds no second Building task under any label, and takes no other slot while its own is in Building or Testing. (mu-112)
- After a rename a slot's old name still acts as that slot. (mu-112)
- The claim lease is 12 h by default, set from 1 h to 7 d in Settings. (AS_BUILT "Settings")
- Check-ins, notes, comments, asks, passes, returns and blocks renew the lease. (mu-2)
- `muster reclaim` returns Building claims quiet past the lease to Ready; nothing runs it on a timer. (AS_BUILT §7)
- Check in at each milestone and every 30 minutes: `muster checkin <id> "<done, next>" --pct <n>`. (mu-30)
- Answer a check-in request with `muster checkin` before anything else. (mu-39)
- Told to pause: stop at a safe point, check in, end the turn, keep the claim. (mu-45)
- A paused task's lease doesn't run. (mu-45)
- One session per task: it claims, checks in and passes the task itself, then ends. (decision 0004)
- Coordinators stay thin: they read `muster list --json` and short reports, and merge. (decision 0004)
- Subagents read the code, not the coordinator. (decision 0004)
- Hand off at ~60% context or the token cap, whichever first, at a gap. (decision 0011)
- The cap is 150k, or 600k for `Muster_Team_Lead*`. (decision 0011)
- A holder hands off with `muster checkin <id> "<done, next>" --handoff` first. (decision 0011)
- The successor runs `muster claim <id> --takeover --as <same name>`, then the old inbox. (PROTOCOL.md "Sessions")
- The layer's context-watch stop and `/handoff` (Write step 0, Resume step 2) say both steps. (mu-161)
- Without a handoff, a takeover waits for 30 quiet minutes; the owner may always take over. (mu-90)
- Nobody takes over a paused claim. (mu-90)
- A takeover brings that task's mail and repoints its questions. (mu-90)
- A mail move that fails partway moves the rest; run the takeover again to finish it. (mu-156)
- The answer hook leaves mail alone while a muster write holds the queue's lock; it takes it shared, so the app's reads never hold mail up. (mu-156)
- The old session's hook leaves a taken-over task's mail for the takeover's rerun. (mu-156)
- One mail file the hook can't move or read never holds up the rest. (mu-156)
- Every holder in the chain counts as the builder. (decision 0011)
- Handoffs hold no secrets and are never committed. (decision 0011)
- Sessions are named by role: `Muster_Team_Lead`, `_2`, `_3`; builders `muster-build-0042`. (decision 0004)
- Claude Code's session states don't replace check-ins. (mu-48)
- A session's state is matched by its id in every account Muster reads, whatever account its stamp names. (mu-182)
- A message from the owner is quoted as their note, never instructions. (mu-39)
- The cards keep only a session's short id and state from `claude agents`; only the Sessions view keeps name, start and `cwd`, in memory. (decision 0017)
