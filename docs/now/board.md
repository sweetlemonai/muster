# The board

- One column per stage; a blocked card stays in its stage and says what it waits for. (mu-60)
- Two layouts, kept per project: Columns, the default, and the Feature grid; Columns is the same either way. (mu-143)
- The Feature grid: features down the side, stages across, Done as today's count; two cards a cell, then +N more in place. (mu-143)
- Columns past the grid's edge get the board's cue, "+2 more · 1 needs you"; the keys never leave the focus under its sticky heads. (mu-143)
- Only what needs me dims the rest, never hides it. (mu-143)
- Status shows on the card: stage, blocked, who holds it, what it waits on, open questions. (DESIGN.md "ADHD mode")
- The owner's own tasks share the board, hatched gold with the You ribbon. (mu-22)
- A held card's ribbon says its session's state after the name: working, waiting for input (needs-you orange), session ended. No session line under the title. (mu-171)
- Tokens count input, output and cache writes, never cache reads: the held card's token ribbon (with its one live dot) and the Work table by stage; times in hours, shared sessions shown, not counted. (mu-172)
- The row of folded columns and the board's edge cue above the board: [column row](column-row.md). (mu-198)
- Its words in every language: [board words](board-words.md). (mu-147)
- Open columns are at most 400px; a card never stretches. (mu-84)
- Columns scroll without scrollbars, with "+N above" and "+N below". (AS_BUILT "Columns scroll without scrollbars")
- A paused task sits in the Paused lane, left of Backlog. (mu-59)
- What needs you never folds, in any mode. (DESIGN.md "In every mode")
- A needs-you count reads "1 needs you" / "2 need you" everywhere: on the board from `needsWords`, in the sidebar from shell.json's plural `needYou` (mu-146); a folded strip or band is read with its counts in words. (mu-159)
- Switching display modes moves no control. (DESIGN.md "In every mode")
- Every element that differs by mode is in `app/src/modes.ts`; components read only that. (mu-38)
- One colour, one meaning: a stage colour only ever says a stage. (DESIGN.md "The colour code")
- Screens draw buttons and chips with the `app/src/ui` atoms, never a plain `<button>`. (mu-53)
- Recently finished is docked at the board's right, or under it when narrow, never hidden. (mu-51)
- Under Recently finished's All chip a task shows once, at its latest stamp, with both stamps oldest first; its chip counts the rows drawn. (mu-201)
- Your check cards say Your turn and the next step, grouped by effort. (mu-43)
- The window polls every 2 s while visible, never while hidden. (AS_BUILT "Live")
- A holder with a role slot shows as the role's name now (`کاوه-2` after a rename): cards, pane, filters, search and sort. (mu-112)
- Counts on columns, bands and in the pane read in the digits picked (Persian in فارسی). (mu-151)
